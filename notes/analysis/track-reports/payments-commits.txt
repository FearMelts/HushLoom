Payments behind a provider adapter: sandbox, CCBill, Segpay, Stripe

Adds src/server/payments/index.ts (paymentProvider, paymentsStatus,
startCheckout), service.ts (processPaymentEvent: exactly-once by
provider+event id, credit grants, subscriptions, refund and chargeback
clawback with credit_debt and chargeback flags, flag listing/clearing,
payment history) and webhook.ts (one handler for every processor).

Providers: a clearly labeled sandbox that refuses production and signs
its own events with a dev-only constant; CCBill FlexForms dynamic
pricing (formDigest) and Webhooks (validation digest, optional URL
token); Segpay eticket pay page and postbacks (postback key, signed
pass-through); Stripe moved behind the adapter, off unless chosen,
with charge.refunded and charge.dispute.created mapped to the shared
clawback. CCBill and Segpay are written from public docs and unverified.

Routes: POST /api/checkout uses the active provider and refuses
chargeback-flagged accounts (403 account_flagged); POST
/api/webhooks/payments/[provider]; /api/webhooks/stripe delegates;
GET/POST /api/admin/flags; POST /api/pricing/sandbox plays the
sandbox processor. Adds CCBILL_WEBHOOK_TOKEN to env.
